About

I'm a founding engineer at Dimely (YC S24), where we're building AI-powered billing automation for B2B SaaS finance teams — agents that read executed contracts and order forms, pull out the commercial terms, flag what doesn't line up, and sync the result into the systems finance already runs on.

Before that I spent two years at BrainBox AI building the data pipelines and testing infrastructure behind a platform that optimizes energy use across thousands of smart buildings — cleaning tens of millions of rows of sensor telemetry a month and keeping the algorithms that feed on it healthy.

Outside of work I build tooling for agentic AI: development pipelines, benchmarks, and infrastructure that let models build and ship software with real guardrails — plus the occasional product when something bugs me enough to fix it.

I'm pursuing an MSCS at Georgia Tech. Before software I studied Accounting & Financial Management at Waterloo, which is where the interest in data and markets first took hold.
